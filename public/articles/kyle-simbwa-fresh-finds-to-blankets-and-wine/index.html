From Fresh Finds to the Blankets & Wine Field: Kyle Simbwa’s Breakout Season

Eight months ago we filed him under Fresh Finds. On December 5 he plays the Blankets & Wine field between Aziz Azion and the headliner. Here is how Kyle Simbwa got here.

When Kyle Simbwa first appeared in our Fresh Finds notes, the pitch was one line: a singer-songwriter who writes like a carpenter — measure twice, one cut. Eight months later he is on the Blankets & Wine Uganda bill, slotted between Aziz Azion and a Nigerian headliner, and the line reads like prophecy.

The catalyst is “Mvule” — a slow-burning afro-folk record named for the hardwood tree, six weeks on our Songs of the Week chart and still top ten. It did what Fresh Finds records are supposed to do: travelled from campus playlists to radio rotation without losing its grain.

Live is where the story closes the loop. Simbwa tours with a three-piece that swaps samples for fingers on strings, and Blankets & Wine — a daylight, seated-on-grass audience — is the exact room his dynamics were built for. Expect “Mvule” extended, expect new material road-tested on the field.

Our Fresh Finds class keeps over-delivering: Etania scores the music policy the same day, and Timothy Code plays a selector slot. The category is not a list; it is a pipeline.
