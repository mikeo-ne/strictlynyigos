Songs of the Week: King Saha’s “Ekibinja” Takes the Crown

A coronation for Ekibinja, a top-three debut for BNXN’s “Kampala Nights”, and Etania still climbing — this is the chart for October 12–18, 2026.

King Saha’s “Ekibinja” spends a second week inside the top two and then does the inevitable: it takes No. 1. The record is built like a rally song — a call, a response, and a drum pattern that behaves like a crowd — and this week the crowd won.

Behind it, Etania’s “Life of the Party” climbs to No. 2, proof that a selector’s single can out-run pure vocalists when the hook is this functional. And the week’s biggest entrance belongs to BNXN, whose “Kampala Nights” debuts at No. 3 off the back of his Blankets & Wine headlining announcement — landings like this are exactly what a headline slot is supposed to do.

Aziz Azion’s seven-week ruler “Zaina” slides to No. 4, King Avo’s “Baddest” re-enters the top five on the strength of the EP, and Zonke Africa’s “Sauti Za Kampala” debuts at No. 10 — the second new entry of the week.

Every song below is one click away from playing, and the full week lives inside our official Spotify playlist, embedded at the bottom of this page. Tap any row for Spotify, or the Apple Music mark on the right of each row for the Apple side.
