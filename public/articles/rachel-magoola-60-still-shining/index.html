Rachel Magoola @60: Still Shining at Kingdom Kampala

The beloved vocalist celebrates her 60th birthday with a full-scale concert on Friday, November 27 — gates at 3 PM, show at 8 PM, tickets from 100K.

Rachel Magoola turns 60, and the plan is not a dinner — it is a concert. “Rachel Magoola @60: Still Shining” lands on Friday, November 27, 2026 at Kingdom Kampala, with gates open at 3 PM and the show starting at 8 PM.

Expect a career-length set: the kadongo kamu lineage she carries, the gospel-inflected ballads that made her a household voice, and the collaborations that introduced her to new generations. A live band, a choir, and a string of guest appearances are billed across the night.

Ticketing is tiered for exactly the room you want: Platinum, a table of 8, at UGX 4M; Gold, a table of 4, at UGX 2M; Silver at UGX 200K; and Bronze at UGX 100K — prime seating and premium service attach to the table tiers.

Tickets are available on Quicket and via MoMo ticketing on *165*20#. Reservations and enquiries: 0772 559 462 / 0704 896 611. Backed by Uganda Tourism Board, Uganda Waragi, Pepsi, Milégé, Capital FM and Beat 96.3 FM, among others — a sponsor wall that tells you how big the night intends to be.
