Songs of the Week: “Zaina” Holds No. 1 as Ekibinja Charges

Aziz Azion’s “Zaina” spends a sixth week on top, King Saha’s “Ekibinja” leaps to No. 2, and Timothy Code debuts — the chart for October 5–11, 2026.

“Zaina” refuses to let go. Aziz Azion’s glide of a record spends a sixth week at No. 1, and the only thing currently threatening it is King Saha’s “Ekibinja”, which jumps from five to two on the strength of club rotation.

Kyle Simbwa’s “Mvule” holds the top three for a fifth week — the Fresh Finds class keeping its chart seat — while Etania’s “Life of the Party” makes the week’s biggest move inside the top five, eight to four.

New this week: Timothy Code’s “Binary Love” debuts at No. 8 and Tai Dai’s “Dye Slow” re-enters at No. 9. City Girl’s “Boss Lady” squeezes in at No. 10 on playlist adds alone.
