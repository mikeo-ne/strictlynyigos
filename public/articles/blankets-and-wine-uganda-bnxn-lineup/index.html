Blankets & Wine Uganda Returns: BNXN Headlines with King Saha, Aziz Azion & More

Nigeria’s BNXN tops the bill on December 5 at Lugogo Rugby Grounds, beside King Saha, Aziz Azion, Kyle Simbwa, Tojo & Sean Offixial and Tai Dai — with a ten-deep music policy behind them.

Pack the blanket, chill the wine: Blankets & Wine Uganda is back, and the bill is the strongest the picnic has ever carried. BNXN flies in from Nigeria to headline on Saturday, December 5, 2026 at the Lugogo Rugby Grounds, joined by King Saha, Aziz Azion, Kyle Simbwa, Tojo & Sean Offixial and Tai Dai.

The headline booking is a statement. BNXN has spent two years turning features into events and events into tours; pairing him with King Saha on the same stage puts Kampala firmly on the circuit he now runs.

Between sets, the day is scored by a music policy — the selectors and vocalists who keep the field moving: Etania “Life of the Party”, DJ Dash, DJ Ssese, Selector Jay, Fem DJ, City Girl, Josh MC, Viana Indi, Timothy Code and Zonke Africa. It is deliberately half-DJ, half-live: blankets do not suit a static crowd.

The ethos is unchanged since the picnic began — bring your own blanket, bring your own wine, keep the field clean. Gates open at noon; the headliner closes the field at sunset. Tickets and table bundles go on sale through Quicket and partner outlets this week.
