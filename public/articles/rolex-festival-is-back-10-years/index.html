Rolex Festival Is Back: Ten Years of Eat, Explore, Compete, Vibe

The Rolex Initiative’s flagship food festival returns to the Cricket Oval on November 29 for its 10th anniversary — ordinary passes at 20K, VIP at 50K.

Ten years in, the Rolex Festival has stopped being a novelty and become a calendar fixture. The Rolex Initiative has confirmed the anniversary edition for Saturday, November 29, 2026 at the Cricket Oval, under the only four words that have ever mattered here: Eat. Explore. Compete. Vibe.

The format is unchanged because it never needed changing: rows of rolex stands and street-food kitchens working at full tilt, eating competitions with genuine stakes, vendor villages, and a stage programme that runs from midday to close. The anniversary edition promises the largest competitor field yet, with regional champions qualifying through outreach rounds.

Passes are deliberately kept democratic — UGX 20,000 for ordinary and UGX 50,000 for a VIP pass with fast-track entry and reserved seating near the competition arena.

Our advice: arrive hungry, leave with a favourite vendor, and do not skip the finals round. For tickets and vendor bookings, call +256 758 939031.
