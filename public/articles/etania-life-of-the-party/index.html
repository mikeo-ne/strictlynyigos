Etania Is the Life of the Party — and She Is Bringing It to Blankets & Wine

The DJ-producer whose single “Life of the Party” is climbing our chart tops the music policy at Blankets & Wine Uganda. A profile of the room-mover behind the tagline.

Every scene has one selector whose name works as a promise. In Kampala right now, that name is Etania, and the promise is the tagline she earned rather than chose: life of the party.

Her sets are engineered escalation — afro house into amapiano into the originals she slips in at peak — and her production carries the same discipline. “Life of the Party”, released October 15, is her first single to chart on our Songs of the Week, and it is climbing: No. 4 last week, No. 2 this week, with the No. 1 slot in sight behind King Saha.

On December 5 she tops the music policy at Blankets & Wine Uganda — the selector crew that scores the field between live sets, alongside DJ Dash, DJ Ssese, Selector Jay, Fem DJ, City Girl, Josh MC, Viana Indi, Timothy Code and Zonke Africa. For a daylight picnic crowd, her brief is specific: keep blankets bouncing without ever tipping into a club set.

“The blanket is the honest dancefloor,” she tells us. “Nobody is standing because the lights say so. If they are up, the record did it.” Watch for her sunset slot — it is the hinge of the whole day.
