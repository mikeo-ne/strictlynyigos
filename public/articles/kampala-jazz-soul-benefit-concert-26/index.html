The Kampala Jazz & Soul Benefit Concert ’26: The Afrigo Band Orchestra Experience

One night, one orchestra, one cause: the Afrigo Band Orchestra Experience takes the UMA Multipurpose Exhibition Hall on October 24, with tables from 3M and tickets from 150K.

The Kampala Jazz & Soul Benefit Concert ’26 returns on Saturday, October 24, 2026, and this year the bill is a single, enormous idea: The Afrigo Band Orchestra Experience — Uganda’s longest-running band, expanded to full orchestral size for one benefit night at the UMA Multipurpose Exhibition Hall (UMA Lugogo).

The benefit angle is the point. Proceeds support the concert’s named charity fund, and the orchestra format means arrangements of Afrigo catalogue classics rebuilt for horns, strings and choir — the kind of versions that exist for exactly one night and then never again.

Seating is table-first, the way a jazz benefit should be: Platinum table of 10 at UGX 8M, Gold table of 8 at 5M, Silver table of 8 at 3M, with single (silver) tickets at 250K and ordinary tickets at 150K for the rows behind the tables.

Dress warm-adjacent, arrive early, and let the hall do its work. For tables and information: 0756939553 / 0789459044.
